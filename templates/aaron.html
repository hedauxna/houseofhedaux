The Room of Aaron
Built in Silence, Wired for Wonder
Aaron’s room is tucked away in a quieter wing of the House of Hedaux, behind a sliding panel that hums open with a soft click. It’s not hidden, exactly—but it doesn’t call attention to itself. Like Aaron, it waits patiently to be discovered.
Chapter I: The Gentle Arrival
Born on 10 January 2006, Aaron was due on New Year’s Day but chose comfort over punctuality. That unhurried entrance set the tone. He didn’t rush then, and he doesn’t rush now. Aaron moves through life at his own pace—quiet, observant, content in his own orbit.
He was never one for noise. While others clamoured for attention, Aaron built worlds in silence. A child of quiet corners and solo adventures, he found joy in the details others missed. His room reflects that: soft lighting, curated shelves, and a sense of calm that feels earned.

Chapter II: The Fashion Frequency
Aaron’s eye for fashion arrived early. Hats, accessories, layered textures—he didn’t follow trends, he created them. His wardrobe was never about fitting in. It was about feeling right. A feathered cap here, a bold pendant there. He dressed like someone who understood that style is a language, and he spoke it fluently. Even now, his outfits are statements. Not loud, but deliberate. A quiet rebellion against the ordinary.

Chapter III: The Obsession Engine
Aaron’s mind is a museum of obsessions. Cars. Mario. Pokémon. Not just casual interests—encyclopedic knowledge. He could name every Pokémon, their traits, their generations, without blinking. His room holds shelves of figures, cards, and plushes, each positioned with precision. Nothing out of place.
Then came the cubes. Rubik’s, speed cubes, 7x7 monsters. He solves them in under a minute, casually. His collection is displayed like art—symmetrical, spotless, sacred. It’s not just about solving. It’s about understanding.

Chapter IV: The Animal Whisperer
Animals love Aaron. Always have. Scooby the dog would melt under his touch. Fudge the rabbit won’t budge when Aaron’s nearby. Even Jody the parrot—famously selective—bows his head for Aaron’s scratches.
There’s something in his energy. A softness. A frequency only animals seem to hear. Maybe he’ll work with them one day. Maybe he already does, in ways we don’t see.

Chapter V: The Digital Den
Aaron’s PC is his kingdom. Valorant is the battlefield. He rages, strategizes, learns. Tournaments play in the background, feeding his gameplay with tips and tactics. His setup glows with RGB pulses, matching his mood. It’s not just a hobby—it’s a craft.
Anime plays on the second screen. Thousands of episodes consumed. Characters memorized. Arcs dissected. His love for Japan runs deep—Nintendo, retro consoles, joypads, language. He plans to visit. Not as a tourist, but as someone returning to a place he’s always felt connected to.

Chapter VI: The Walks and the Walls
Aaron walks.
Maybe for fitness. Not for errands. Just walks. Sometimes for hours. No destination, no explanation. He slips out quietly, returns just as quietly. You ask where he went—he shrugs. You ask what he saw—“Not much.” But something always shifts in him after. Like the world outside helped him rearrange something inside. His walks are part ritual, part retreat. A way to reset. To think. Or maybe not to think at all.
Conversation with Aaron is minimal. “How was your day?” earns a “Sure.” But when the mood is right—when the stars align or the drink flows—he opens. And there he is. Dry wit, sharp timing, a sense of humour that catches you off guard. It’s like finding a secret room behind a bookshelf. You didn’t know it was there, but now you never want to leave.
Aaron is a mystery. Not because he hides, but because he doesn’t explain. His room reflects that. It doesn’t offer answers. It offers space. And sometimes, that’s more revealing than words.

Chapter VII: The Heat of It All
Aaron loves food. Not just any food—the hotter the better. Curries that make your eyes water. Noodles that bite back. He’s drawn to spice like it’s a challenge, a thrill, a flavour that matches his quiet intensity. His room has a tucked-away drawer of hot sauces, ramen packets, and mystery snacks with labels in Japanese. It’s not a shrine—it’s a stash. And it’s sacred.
Food, like fashion, is another way Aaron expresses himself. Quietly. Boldly. Without compromise.

Chapter VIII: The Mystery Drawer
There’s a drawer in Aaron’s room. Locked. No key. No clues. Maybe it holds the answer to what he’ll become. Maybe it’s just a metaphor. Either way, it belongs there. Aaron’s future is unwritten. Full of mystery. But if his past is anything to go by, it’ll be something great. Something different. Something unmistakably Aaron.
